Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

🔒 Short version: Your images never leave your device. We collect no personal data, use no tracking cookies, and have no servers that process your files.

1. No Data Collection

Honor Image Solutions does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or images. All image processing happens entirely within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your files never reach any server — because we have none involved in image processing.

2. No Cookies or Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party tracking scripts. The only data we store is your dark/light mode preference, saved locally in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device.

3. No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer any user data to any third party — because we simply do not collect any.

4. External Libraries

When you export multiple images, we load the JSZip library from Cloudflare CDN to package your files into a ZIP. This is a standard open-source library. No image data or personal information is sent to Cloudflare during this process.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, this is not a concern in practice.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
